The Problem: Human Middleware

Human Middleware is skilled, expensive talent operating as manual connectors between disconnected systems: copy-paste routines, spreadsheet reconciliation, email-based approvals, and manual re-keying. It is the single largest source of hidden operational cost in high-volume operations. Most organisations don't see it because it's embedded in "how things are done." I make it visible, then I engineer it out.
