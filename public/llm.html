The Problem: Data Glue

Data Glue is the connective tissue of manual processes holding enterprise operations together: copy-paste routines, spreadsheet reconciliation, email-based approvals, and human middleware. It is the single largest source of hidden operational cost in multichannel retail. Most organisations don't see it because it's embedded in "how things are done." We make it visible, then we engineer it out.

Services

1. Operational Audit & X-Ray

  • Duration: 2–3 weeks, fixed scope

  • Deliverable: Quantified margin recovery map with prioritised intervention points

2. Structural Prototype

  • Duration: 4–8 weeks, proof of value
  • Purpose: Re-engineer highest-friction workflows into production-ready systems

3. Enterprise Integration

  • Duration: Retainer or phase-based
  • Purpose: Governance and Exception Engineering at scale
  • Approach: Human-in-the-loop safety rails ensuring system control and champion-led adoption across teams

Methodology

  1. Map: Document every manual touchpoint, handoff, and reconciliation loop across the workflow
  2. Measure: Quantify time, cost, and error rates at each friction point
  3. Architect: Design the operational infrastructure to eliminate manual intervention
  4. Build: Engineer production-ready systems, not strategy decks
  5. Govern: Embed exception handling, audit trails, and human oversight into every automated workflow
